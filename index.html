P1 Paid advertising

Limited academy credit, and grades that don't depend on spend

A learner who spends their own money gets no higher grade. Assessment measures method: setup, tracking, testing and analysis.

  1. 1Simulation · freeBuild campaigns, read reports and optimize on prepared data. This is the basis of IM205 assessment.
  2. 2Live campaign · academy creditIn P1, teams of 3 share one credit. In Track C, each learner gets individual credit, and most of the budget goes there.
  3. 3Client budget · AG302Real campaigns inside P2 client projects, included in the case study.
  4. 4Personal spend · optionalNot graded. Requires a signed acknowledgement and a suggested monthly cap, and applies only to the learner's own product or brand.

AI policy

We use AI in production, and learners explain and defend their work

  • Learners log tools, prompts, references, first outputs, human edits, reasons for choices and usage rights
  • Specific individual assessments without AI check understanding of design, editing and marketing
  • Client data protection, consent from people in images and voices, and respect for intellectual property
  • No misleading content and no plagiarism
